## ✅ How I work

- **Measure, then trust.** I validate hardware with real data and AI-assisted designs with proper tools. I draft PCB schematics with AI help, then verify every one in KiCad.
- **Safety first.** Daily work in electrical safety testing taught me to find the failure mode before it ships, and to share what I learn with the design team.
- **Explain it simply.** Teaching programming to kids and presenting to non-specialists keeps my documentation and demos clear.
- **Verifiable record.** My degrees, publications, and employment history are all listed on my portfolio, Google Scholar, and LinkedIn.
